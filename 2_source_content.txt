Network Design.ai  - Context and Problem Statement
The sales cycles in telecom companies take several weeks, for enterprise and SMB customers, 
where customer requirements need to be understood, solution designed, order created, 
provisioned and sent for billing. 
Why target network design?

•
Time-Consuming: Lengthy manual design process, 
leading to delays in project timelines and extended 
sales cycles. This can result in lost opportunities and 
revenue leakage.
•
Human Error: Manual designs are prone to errors due 
to oversight or miscommunication among team 
members. According to a study by Gartner, human 
errors can account for up to 70% of network outages, 
leading to increased costs and customer 
dissatisfaction.
•
Scalability Issues: As the demand for network services 
grows, scaling manual design processes becomes 
increasingly difficult
•
Inflexibility: Manual processes can lack the agility 
needed to adapt to changing customer requirements 
or new technologies. This inflexibility can hinder the 
ability to deliver customized solutions quickly.
•
Lack of Standardization: Different teams may use 
varying methodologies and tools for network design, 
leading to inconsistencies and difficulties in 
maintaining quality across projects.
